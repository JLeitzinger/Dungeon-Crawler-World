There's no Armor Class in this game — armor doesn't add a flat number to anything. Instead, armor grants (or improves) the Defend skill, the same way weapons grant combat skills like Slash (see [[Skill Acquisition]]).

## How It Works
- Armor grants Defend only while worn, the same way a weapon only grants its skill while equipped.
- Better armor grants a higher Defend level, meaning more dice when you use it to defend (see [[Skill Improvements]]).
- A crawler wearing no armor has no Defend skill from gear at all — they fall back to whatever they'd normally defend with ([[Do Something]], or an Acrobatics/Dodge-type skill if they have one).

## Using It
Defend is just a skill like any other. When an enemy attacks you (see [[Attack]]), you choose what skill to defend with — if your armor grants you Defend, that's usually your best option; if you're unarmored, you might lean on Acrobatics or Dodge instead.

## For Content Authors
When creating armor in DCW-Content, grant Defend instead of a flat `acBonus` — light/basic armor around Defend 1, heavier or masterwork pieces up to Defend 2-3, matching the same scaling weapons already use for their granted skills.

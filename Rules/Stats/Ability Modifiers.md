Every ability score has a modifier, using the same formula as the d20 games this borrows from:

`mod = floor((score - 10) / 2)`

Ability modifiers are a flat bonus, not extra dice. They never change your dice pool size — only your skill level does that (see [[Skill Improvements]]).

This formula applies to the six rolled abilities only. [[LUK]] is the exception — it has no rolled score and no modifier formula. Its raw value is added directly to every roll instead.

## Skill Checks
When you make a skill check, your total is:

(sum of the dice rolled, one d6 per level of the skill) + (the flat modifier of that skill's related ability)

For example, a level 2 skill related to STR rolls 2d6 and adds your STR modifier once to the total.

## Raw Ability Checks
Some checks aren't tied to a specific skill (e.g. a Constitution check to resist poison, or to stabilize while [[Dying]]). These use your [[Do Something]] pool — 1d6, since Do Something never levels up — plus the relevant ability's modifier. If you have a skill that's clearly related (e.g. Fortitude for a Constitution check), use that skill's pool instead of Do Something's.
